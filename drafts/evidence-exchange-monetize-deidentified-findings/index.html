Closer clinicians.
Stronger evidence. Shared value.

Once clinicians trust a finding, Clinical Token AI agents help package it as de-identified evidence for the Clinical Token Evidence Exchange, a secure, HIPAA-aligned network for professional collaboration. Peers can review the case pattern, discuss interpretation, and compare notes without exposing patient identity. Clinics can also participate in responsible value exchange around real-world clinical intelligence, keeping PHI off the shared surface.

Our vision is simple: bring clinicians closer to clinicians, clinicians closer to patients through better-informed care conversations, and medtech and other healthcare partners into one trusted evidence layer. The Exchange supports peer learning and professional judgment. It does not replace the clinician, and it is not offered as a diagnostic device claim.
